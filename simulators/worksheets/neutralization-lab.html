Part C · From full equations to net ionic equations

  1. Choose HCl + NaOH. Copy the three equations from the Equations panel.
    Full: ____________________________________________________________
    Full ionic: _______________________________________________________
    Net ionic: ________________________________________________________
    Spectator ions: ______________. Why are they called spectators?
  2. Now choose HCl + KOH and HNO₃ + NaOH. What do you notice about the net ionic equation every time? Explain why.
  3. Choose CH₃COOH + NaOH. Write the net ionic equation. Why is ethanoic acid not split into H⁺ and CH₃COO⁻ ions?
  4. Choose HCl + CaCO₃. Why is CaCO₃ written as (s) and not split into ions? Write the net ionic equation.
  5. Choose H₂SO₄ + Ca(OH)₂. The simulator says there are no spectator ions. Explain why.
  6. Use the toggle to switch from H⁺(aq) to H₃O⁺(aq). Rewrite the net ionic equation for HCl + NaOH using H₃O⁺.
