Part C · Boundaries are not walls

  1. Select Al₂O₃ and then AlCl₃. Record χ̄ and Δχ for each. Both contain aluminium, so why are they in different regions?
    Substanceχ̄ΔχRegionMelting point

  2. Do the melting points support the positions on the triangle? Explain.
  3. Select GaAs. It is a semiconductor used in solar cells and LEDs. Where does it sit, and how does its position fit its properties?

Part D · Limits of the model

  1. Select LiH. Where does the triangle place it? In fact LiH is an ionic solid containing Li⁺ and H⁻ ions. Suggest why the model gets this one wrong.
  2. Switch between 1 d.p. and 2 d.p. values. Does the predicted region for NaCl change? When might the extra precision matter?
  3. The model uses only which two elements are present. List two things it ignores that also affect bonding.
