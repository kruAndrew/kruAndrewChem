Part D · Seeing the shift: N₂O₄ ⇌ 2NO₂ (ΔH = +57 kJ)

  1. N₂O₄ is colourless and NO₂ is brown. Predict the colour change when the temperature is increased, then test it. Explain using ΔH.
  2. Increase the pressure. Describe the colour change in two stages and explain each stage.

Part E · Catalysts and industry

  1. Add a catalyst, then add N₂. Compare how quickly the lines level off with your earlier test. Why does a catalyst not change the position of equilibrium?
  2. In the Contact process (2SO₂ + O₂ ⇌ 2SO₃, ΔH = −198 kJ), a low temperature gives a higher yield, but industry uses about 450 °C. Explain this compromise.
  3. Exam-style. State and explain the effect of decreasing the pressure on the equilibrium yield of SO₃. [2]
