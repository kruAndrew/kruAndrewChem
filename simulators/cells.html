Voltaic and Electrolytic Cells

Build a cell and watch the electrons move through the wire. In a voltaic cell a spontaneous reaction pushes electrons round the circuit. In an electrolytic cell a power supply forces a non-spontaneous reaction to happen.

Standard electrode potentials used here (from the data booklet)

The more negative E⦵, the more readily the metal is oxidized, so the more reactive the metal. In a voltaic cell, the half-cell with the more negative E⦵ is the anode.
