Model assumptions: particles have no volume and no attractions, and they bounce off the walls without losing energy. Collisions between particles are perfectly elastic: individual particles speed up and slow down, but the total kinetic energy, and so the temperature, stays the same. The particles are drawn with a small size so you can see them collide; this has a negligible effect on pressure. Speeds are for nitrogen. R = 8.31 J K⁻¹ mol⁻¹, and kPa × dm³ = J.
