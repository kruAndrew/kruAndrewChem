* % ionic character uses Pauling's estimate, 100(1 − e−Δχ²/4); textbooks give slightly different curves. Region boundaries are approximate: ionic from Δχ ≈ 1.8 upwards (a common rule of thumb), and a sloping metallic/covalent divide near χ̄ ≈ 2.0 that runs through the metalloids. The model uses only which two elements are present, not the formula, oxidation state or structure, so substances near a boundary (Al₂O₃, AlCl₃, GaAs) show mixed character, and some, such as LiH, land in the "wrong" region.
