Full mock papers

Complete practice papers in the IB format. Paper 1 has two parts in one sitting: 1A is multiple choice and 1B is data-based questions. Paper 2 has short-answer and extended-response questions. Time yourself and use the data booklet.

Simulators

Interactive models to explore and practise with. They work on laptops, tablets and phones. Each one has a 2-page worksheet (📄) that walks you through the concept and how the settings link together. Open it beside the simulator, or print it on A4.
